# Reflection — write this yourself, without AI

Write approximately half a page in your own words after doing the fieldwork and building/testing the tool.

Prompts:
- What did I get wrong at the start?
- What would I cut from the project if I had less time?
- Where did AI help me?
- Where did AI waste time or make an incorrect assumption?
- What did the staff member say that changed my plan?
- What should the tool NOT be trusted for?

Do not submit AI-written reflection text. Replace these prompts with your own honest reflection.
