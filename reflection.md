1. What did I get wrong at the start?
- I underestimated the amount of food wastage.
- I also underestimated how many students were absent during dinner.
2. What would I cut if I had less time?
- Tracking night-canteen attendance would be the first feature I would reduce or postpone.
3. Where did AI help me?
- Structuring my thought process.
- Identifying missing edge cases and possible scenarios.
- Writing code and helping with testing.
4. Where did AI waste time?
- Overcomplicating the solution.
- Focusing on optimisation that was unnecessary during the early stages.
5. What did I learn from the fieldwork?
- The mess supervisor highlighted food wastage, inaccurate human-based estimates and financial losses.
- The actual food wastage was higher than I expected.
6. What should this tool not be trusted for?
- Predictions should not be treated as constant or guaranteed values.
- They are estimates based on historical observations and should support, not replace, human decisions.
